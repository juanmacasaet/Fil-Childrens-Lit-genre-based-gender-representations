# Fil-Childrens-Lit-genre-based-gender-representations

This is the file directory of the source code and outputs of the study named Evaluating Use of Language Models on Gender Representation in Genre-Based Filipino Children's Literature

The main source code file is FCL-genre-analysis-main.ipynb

## Abstract

This study makes use of a genre-based approach in understanding gender representation in \textit{Filipino Children's Literature} (FCL). Using publicly available online materials, the researched focused on analyzing gender associations in FCL written in Filipino. Specifically the study looked into gender associations across genres of Fiction, Traditional/Contemporary Literature, and Creative Nonfiction, with the use of static and dynamic word and sentence embedding models. Word2Vec was used for static word embeddings and Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT) for dynamic word embeddings. Association metrics such as Relative Norm Distance (RND) and Word Embedding Association Test (WEAT) were implemented on the static word embeddings, while Sentence Encoder Association Test (SEAT) on the dynamic sentence embeddings. Findings from the RND associations suggest a favorable shift of women's representation in FCL within genres of Fiction and Traditional / Contemporary Literature. SEAT results showed that the BERT dynamic sentence embeddings produced significant results on how gender differs across genre groups and social meanings from Fiction and Traditional / Contemporary Literature texts compared to the Word2Vec static word embeddings in terms of their effect sizes and p-values. Viewing gender as Indexicality, Stance, and Performativity, shows how gender representations are shaped by associations of linguistic forms with particular social meanings, behaviors, and roles with gendered identities. This study lays the foundation in further explorations on gender representation through computational linguistics.
